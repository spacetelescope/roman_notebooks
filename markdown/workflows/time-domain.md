# RRN Science Workflows: Time Domain

This science workflow guides users through simulating and analyzing a time-variable source in Roman WFI images. The current notebooks use a Type Ia supernova and supernova-specific light-curve modeling. The broader simulation and photometry pattern can be adapted to other transients and variable sources.

## Workflow Overview

1. [**Time Domain Simulations**](../../notebooks/time_domain_simulations/time_domain_simulations.ipynb)

   Simulate a time series of Roman WFI images containing a Type Ia supernova and its host galaxy with Roman I-Sim. Generate a model light curve with `sncosmo`, inject the source at each observing epoch, create calibrated Level 2 images and cutouts, and visualize the evolving transient.

2. [**Time Domain Analysis**](../../notebooks/time_domain_analysis/time_domain_analysis.ipynb)

   Measure the transient at its known position using background-subtracted aperture photometry and PSF-fitting photometry. Compare the methods and their signal-to-noise diagnostics, construct a light curve, and fit its normalized amplitude and peak time. This notebook can use cutouts created by the simulation notebook or generate lightweight demonstration cutouts when those products are unavailable.

## Caveats and Limitations

- The analysis uses forced photometry at the known position of the injected transient; it does not perform transient detection.
- The simulated scene and observing sequence are simplified and are not intended to reproduce the full complexity of a Roman time-domain survey.
- The lightweight cutouts generated by the analysis notebook are provided for demonstration only.
- The reported photometric uncertainties and signal-to-noise values are diagnostic estimates rather than a final uncertainty model.
